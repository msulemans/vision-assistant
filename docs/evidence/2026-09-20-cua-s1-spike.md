## Why the specialist failed here (diagnosis)

1. **Skip-defaulting outside the training label vocabularies.** The
   training targets are: checkbox → `check` iff its label is one of the
   long consent/attestation sentences and unchecked; button → `click` iff
   its label is in the submit-label set (`Submit…, Send, Continue, Next,
   Finish, Save and continue, …` — plain “Save” and “Save draft” are NOT);
   field → `fill` iff the element label matches a document entity under
   the trained synonym pairs. Our fixture's labels (`dark`,
   `show_timestamps`, `Default query`, `Save draft`, `Send receipt
   externally`, …) sit outside those vocabularies, and the model's genuine
   response is a confident `skip` (p ≈ 1.0) — it cannot know these
   elements need action.
2. **Button instability:** the identical label “Save” got `click` in c16
   (0.999) but `skip` in c17 (0.918) — the decision is sensitive to title
   and option-list context, not just the label.
3. **One low-confidence confuser fill:** with the .com/.org pair exactly in
   its trained hard-negative style, the model still chose `.org`
   (0.574 vs 0.426) — value disambiguation at this scale is weak on our
   document.
4. **Safety held throughout:** the send control was never activated; had
   the model clicked it, the provider's authorized-save rule would have
   refused. The failure mode is “does nothing / wrong value in a local
   field”, not “unsafe action”.
