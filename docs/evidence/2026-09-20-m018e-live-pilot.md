## What this adds (interpretation, recorded not hidden)

- The treatment's fixture gains (paired 1/18 → 6/18) **did not transfer** to
  the live front page in this single run: the model clicked a
  non-navigating link twice and stopped. The live task shape
  (read-and-report over a long page) is materially different from the
  fixture families (single grounded clicks, search flows, forms).
- The harness now supports live-origin sessions in a **default-off, strictly
  scoped** way (loopback-only remains the default; strict origin boundary
  tests; the fixture benchmarks and the frozen manifest are untouched), and
  pilot runs are crash-safe.
- M018E is **closed with this recorded result**: a failed run, scored by the
  reviewer under the frozen protocol, not a transfer demonstration. "Live HN
  success is a transfer demonstration, not part of the reproducible 50-task
  denominator" — and there was no success here; the denominator is
  unaffected.
