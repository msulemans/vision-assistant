## Failure anatomy (three distinct model-side classes)

1. **Loop-discipline stall (3/5 tasks).** After an advance, the model
   repeatedly emitted `no_candidates` on the same observation instead of
   `next_viewport` (d22-01: calls 4–17, one legal action each — no refusal
   fired, so the recovery counter never engaged and the call budget ended
   the run). A future harness revision could count consecutive
   no-progress calls against recovery; per the freeze, nothing was changed
   after the run.
2. **Classification errors (both finished tasks).** Decoys were marked
   `primary`: "Standard ML in production compilers" (a programming
   language) and "Neural tube development" (biology) — the classic
   token-trap failure. True primaries without obvious AI keywords in the
   title were missed (d22-04 rank 1 "Sparse attention, cheap inference";
   d22-05 rank 33 landed in the ledger but was displaced by the decoy).
3. **Reading precision.** Titles were transcribed with the displayed
   "(domain)" suffix appended (e.g. "…what they miss (evals.example)") —
   visible-page data, but not the title field.

Validation behaved exactly as designed throughout: an over-long reason on
the first proposal was refused with feedback and retried; duplicate
candidates were rejected without overwriting; the trusted selection took
the model's three lowest `primary` entries and the final `ranks` matched
them exactly.

## Verdict against the frozen plan

The plan stated: *if a five-task development smoke still cannot combine
viewport evidence, then a stronger model is justified — with this harness
as the fair test.* The smoke failed the gate, but the evidence shows the
combination premise held (multi-observation selections were actually
produced). The remaining walls are generalist-4B capability walls:
fine-grained classification under decoys, exact transcription, and bounded
loop discipline. Both readings point the same way for next steps:

- for the generalist screenshot lane: a **stronger model** under the same
  frozen harness is the justified variable, or
- for bounded form decisions: **specialist scoring models** (see
  `docs/CUA_S1_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`, inspection-only) that never generate
  text or choose targets, with this project's control plane unchanged.
